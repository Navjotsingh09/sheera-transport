Job Information

Requirements

The requirements below apply to the multi-drop parcel delivery vacancies. The HelloFresh vacancy has its own requirements.

Licence Held for Two Years

A UK or EU driving licence, held for at least two years.

Driving Record

No more than six penalty points and no convictions for drink driving or driving without insurance.

Age and Experience

You must be aged 23 or over. No CV or delivery experience is required; paid training is provided.

Physical Requirements

You must be able to load and deliver parcels weighing up to 23kg.

The Work

Multi-drop rounds are approximately nine hours. You will use a device for navigation and delivery records, and provide professional customer service.

HelloFresh Contract

Full UK licence, reliable attendance, customer service skills, independent working and physical handling of delivery boxes. Previous delivery experience is preferred, not essential.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous delivery experience?

No. Full paid training is provided.

What licence do I need?

A UK or EU driving licence held for at least two years.

Do I need my own van?

No. Van options are available including insurance.

When do I get paid?

Weekly pay starts from week three onwards.

Is fuel included?

Fuel allowance is paid on top of your daily rate.

How many days can I work?

Up to six days per week on a rotating schedule.

Is work available after Christmas?

Subject to location and operational requirements, many routes continue throughout the year.
