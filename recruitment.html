Requirements

What we're looking for in our drivers

Valid Licence

Full UK or EU driving licence held for at least 2 years.

Clean Record

Max 6 penalty points. No convictions for drink-driving or driving uninsured.

Age & Capability

Aged 23 or over. Physically capable of handling packages up to 23kg.

Navigation

Basic navigation skills and confidence operating electronic delivery devices.

Professionalism

Friendly, customer-focused attitude and commitment to safety.

Reliability

Punctual, dependable, and committed to complete delivery rounds.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous delivery experience?

No. Full paid training is provided.

What licence do I need?

A UK or EU driving licence held for at least two years.

Do I need my own van?

No. Van options are available including insurance.

When do I get paid?

Weekly pay starts from week three onwards.

Is fuel included?

Fuel allowance is paid on top of your daily rate.

How many days can I work?

Up to six days per week on a rotating schedule.

Is work available after Christmas?

Subject to location and operational requirements, many routes continue throughout the year.
